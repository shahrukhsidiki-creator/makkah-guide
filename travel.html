📋 Before you fly

  • Check your visa route and whether it permits Umrah for your nationality — the rules have been changing.
  • Meningitis ACWY certificate, and whatever else is currently required at entry.
  • Book the Rawdah permit for Madinah through Nusuk as soon as dates are fixed — slots go fast.
  • Unscented toiletries, bought at home. You will not find them easily in a hurry.
  • Tell your bank you're travelling, and carry some cash — not every small shop takes cards.
  • Download this site to your home screen so it works without signal.
